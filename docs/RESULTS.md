# Results

Results from the saved experiment records linked below. Earlier component tables were generated by `uv run python -m riskengine.eval` on CPU. Test rules and later experiment rules are linked separately; no evaluation was rerun for this update. See the [experiment log](EXPERIMENTS.md).

## Current configuration and saved signals

[default.toml](../config/default.toml) now selects `sentiment = "entity"` and `events = "model_rules"`. New pipeline scoring uses E27's company-name/headline pair model and computes impact per company. Missing company names fall back to the older `own` headline model; `own` and `finbert` remain selectable.

The default and the saved replay have different sentiment provenance. `data/outputs/signals.jsonl.gz` still contains `own` sentiment and is kept for the credit chain. The separate `signals_entity.jsonl.gz` supplied the entity-sentiment Module A comparison below. Changing the config did not replace the saved credit signals.

## Label provenance

All labels newly created for the project's event and credit work are **AI-made or AI-adjudicated, with no human check**. Of the new reference sets listed here, only SEntFiN uses human-made labels, supplied by its dataset authors.

| Reference | Label source | Human check of project-created labels |
|---|---|---|
| E24 D1/D2 development and final news/tweet sets | ChatGPT, Claude and AI adjudication | None |
| Macro/Geo adjudication | ChatGPT, Claude and AI resolution | None |
| Wider 8-K credit set | ChatGPT and Claude; Claude's label used on disagreements | None |
| SEntFiN for E27 | Dataset authors' human-made labels | No new project labels were created |

Provenance values include `ai_agreed`, `ai_agreed_ai_audited` and `ai_adjudicated`. An AI audit or adjudication is not a human check. In the wider credit set, the adjudicator was also one of the labellers, so resolution was not independent. The [annotation protocol](ANNOTATION_PROTOCOL.md) describes intended quality controls; it does not establish that human review happened. The [E24 labelling amendment](E25_LABELLER_AMENDMENT.md) records the AI-label process. Older third-party benchmarks below are separate from these new project annotations.

## Module B alert gates

### Credit reference sets

The wider 8-K set contains **287 headlines** sampled around bankruptcy/acceleration filings. Item codes are sampling anchors, not headline labels. ChatGPT and Claude agreed on **249/287 (86.8%)**; Claude's label was used on the 38 disagreements. The adjudicator was therefore also a labeller. All labels are AI-made or AI-adjudicated, with **no human check**.

| Credit-gate evaluation | Rows | Recall | Precision |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Wider 8-K set, frozen C | 287 | 0.871 | 0.842 |
| E23 | — | — | 0.47 |

On the 8-K set there were 147 credit-labelled rows and 152 predicted credit rows: 128 true alerts, 24 false alerts and 19 misses. Provenance counts are 169 `ai_agreed`, 80 `ai_agreed_ai_audited` and 38 `ai_adjudicated`. Using ChatGPT's labels instead gives recall 0.921 and precision 0.770, showing sensitivity to label choice. Source: [credit result](../data/outputs/e25_credit_8k_full_gate.json).

**These sets are not comparable.** The 8-K sample is Default-heavy; its 0.842 precision does not establish an improvement over E23. E23's 0.47 remains the reported worst case. Neither the sampling scheme nor the AI adjudication establishes population performance.

### Entity sentiment can miss a downgrade

On the replay, switching to entity sentiment reduced credit alerts **4 to 2**. A false BAC alert from an India-stocks headline was correctly removed. A real Boeing downgrade was also lost: sentiment changed from -0.544 to -0.029 and impact from 8 to 6, below the trigger of 7. Entity sentiment can under-read a downgrade headline. The credit chain therefore keeps the stored `own`-sentiment signals. This is a replay check, not a new independent credit test. Source: [credit-alert comparison in the experiment log](EXPERIMENTS.md#entity-sentiment-and-the-credit-alerts-2026-10-18).

### Market gate

A separate gate handles Geopolitical/Macroeconomic events. On the stored `own`-sentiment replay after the switch to C, it produced **15 alerts**. About 70% looked market-wide in an earlier non-blind read of the 16 alerts before the switch. That read was exploratory; it does not establish precision for the current 15 alerts. Source: [E26 and engine-switch entries](EXPERIMENTS.md).

Do not adopt v3 as an extra non-credit trigger; keep the credit and market-wide gates.

**Why not:** at the same number of alerts, v3 was no better than simply choosing the most volatile stocks (P99 precision 0.076 vs 0.067 in 2019, 0.411 vs 0.428 in 2020; the difference interval includes 0 in both years). So v3 ranks headlines well (Spearman, see `IMPACT_V3.md`), but for alerts it adds nothing beyond "this stock is volatile". Module B therefore stays on the credit gate (precision and recall measured above) and the market-wide gate. The usual "impact formula >= 7 or 8" trigger is worse still (recall 0.02-0.03).

A big price move validates "the market reacted", not "it was a crisis".

## Entity-aware sentiment (E27)

The registered test used **SEntFiN Indian business news with human-made labels from the dataset authors**. A scores the headline once and copies the sentiment to every named entity; B uses (company name, headline) pairs with per-row int8 ONNX inference. The test was run once after dev-based model selection.

| Test subset | Entity rows | A: headline macro-F1 | B: entity macro-F1 | B - A | 95% range of gain |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---|
| Conflicting headlines | 463 | 0.476 | 0.821 | +0.344 | 0.295 to 0.400 |
| All test rows | 2,152 | 0.675 | 0.864 | +0.189 | 0.169 to 0.211 |

The fixed rule required at least +0.05 macro-F1 on conflicting rows and no all-test drop greater than 0.02. Both conditions passed. Intervals use 1,000 paired entity-row bootstrap resamples, seed 0. The [saved report](../data/outputs/e27_entity_eval.json) predates activation and records the recommendation without applying it; the current config now selects `entity`. The rule is in [E27_ENTITY_SENTIMENT.md](E27_ENTITY_SENTIMENT.md).

Only the winning seed's record was downloaded, so the comparison across all training seeds cannot be rechecked. Training on SEntFiN also spends its earlier E20 role as an unused confirmation set. The registered E27 result covers Indian news headlines, not broad US news or tweet performance.
